A new era in continuous biomanufacturing

Deep·biotech
Stämm is a biotechnology company revolutionizing the production of biologics and cell therapies through continuous and automated manufacturing. Their proprietary bioprocessor enables scalable, decentralized, and cost-effective biomanufacturing by integrating advanced bioengineering and optimal control of environmental conditions.

The future of biologicals, powered by living fossils

Agri·food·land use
Puna Bio harnesses extremophile bacteria that have lived on Earth for 3.5 billion years to create the world’s first extremophile bioinoculant. Their products boost crop yields by 10–15% in fertile soils and enable growth in degraded or extreme environments.

CRISPR-powered diagnostics for rapid, decentralized healthcare

Human health
CASPR Biotech pioneers next-generation molecular diagnostics by harnessing CRISPR and novel Cas enzymes. Their portable, low-cost platform enables rapid detection of infectious diseases and antimicrobial resistance, delivering results in under an hour without the need for centralized laboratories.

A new paradigm in crop pollination

Agri·food·land use
Beeflow is a biotechnology company that provides professional pollination services to farmers for maximizing crop yield potential and fruit quality. They developed a platform of proprietary technologies to address pollination challenges as well as pollination efficiency data analysis for managing pollination during blooming.

Replacing petrochemical food dyes with fungal ones

Agri·food·land use
Michroma is creating the next generation of natural ingredients by harnessing the power of fungi, nature's most efficient biofactory. Using advanced bioinformatics and synthetic biology, Michroma designs high-performance microbial platforms to produce novel ingredients in a sustainable and cost-effective manner.

Redefining drug discovery by starting with patients

Human health
OncoPrecision uses a first-principles approach to address the biological complexity of cancer drug development. By leveraging patient-derived data early in the process, they build a pipeline of therapeutic antibodies aimed at areas of high unmet clinical need—ensuring more targeted, effective treatments before reaching the clinic.

Green solvents engineered from natural compounds

Bio·Industry
Their proprietary technology enables the creation of high-performance, bio-based eutectic solvents using natural compounds—turning solids into functional liquids for applications across food, cosmetics, pharma, and beyond.

Transforming health with Single-domain Antibodies

Human health
Nanogrow is developing a new generation of biological treatments that combine the efficacy of antibodies with the safety of localized delivery, expanding what traditional mAbs can do. Their unique nanobody platform integrates natural and synthetic libraries with advanced computational tools—AI, structural modeling, and biophysical analysis—to design optimal candidates and tailor delivery to each therapeutic need. This approach improves binding, stability, and manufacturability while reducing development time and costs.
