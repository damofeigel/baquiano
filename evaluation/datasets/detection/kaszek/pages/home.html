Technology is our core. We invest across industries, backing founders who use technology to create large new markets or fundamentally improve existing ones, building solutions for both consumers and enterprises. Our investments span financial services, healthcare, education, ecommerce, marketplaces and software, among other sectors.

We believe AI will expand and accelerate these opportunities, transforming how people interact with technology and how products are built and businesses operate. In Latin America, we see particular promise at the application layer, where founders can address local needs with better solutions and tackle previously intractable problems, and at the infrastructure layer, where the region’s abundant energy resources and proximity to the United States create opportunities to support growing global demand for computing capacity.

Our early-stage funds make initial investments from pre-seed and seed through Series A and Series B. We partner with founders at a wide range of starting points—from a promising idea to a business gaining traction and scaling—and aim to support them through multiple rounds as their companies grow.

Our opportunity funds invest from Series C through pre-IPO financing, extending our ability to partner with entrepreneurs throughout their journeys.

We mostly invest in companies that target Latin America as their main initial market.

We strongly believe in the enormous opportunity that the technology ecosystem presents in the region, and in our capacity to add significant value across Latin America, since it is an ecosystem we have been involved with for more than 20 years as entrepreneurs, mentors, and investors.

We look forward to partnering with visionary and inspirational founders, who are honest, determined, and purpose driven.

Entrepreneurs that know with clarity not only what they want to accomplish, but also why they want to do it.  That always see the glass half-full, relentlessly search for the light at the end of the tunnel, and challenge all boundaries to bend reality and change the world.

At a more personal level, we love founders that are strong leaders AND great team players, with product-oriented mindsets, and who dare to embrace the unknown.
