HOW IT WORKS

Invest. Integrate. Scale.

Most VCs select and wait. Kamay pairs disciplined selection with a corporate engine that turns real operating problems into signed contracts — what we call Dual Alpha: selection, plus commercial acceleration.

THE CHECK

We invest at Seed and Pre-Series A. Selection is where the investment starts. Our operating model is designed to continue beyond it.

THE INTEGRATION

Kamay doesn't stop at selection. The same operating engine can help portfolio companies move from corporate access to validation, PoCs, contracts and scale — 226 PoCs designed and 32 contracts signed and running across the ecosystem.

THE SCALE

Distribution is Latin America's scarce asset. It is not a relationship that can walk out the door. Approximately 6 million points of sale, 305 production plants and 1,900 distribution centres across our partners' operations.
