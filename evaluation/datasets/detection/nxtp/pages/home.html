Nuvemshop is Latin America's leading e-commerce platform. It currently empowers 85,000+ merchants to launch, develop and promote their own online businesses. A Seed investment from our Fund I, Nuvemshop recently announced a $500m Series E in August 2021, led by Insight Partners and Tiger Global.
